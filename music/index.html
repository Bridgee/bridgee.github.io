About My Music

I'm a composer, sound designer, and music synthesizer designer/experimenter who leverages my EE background in signal processing and circuit design. Sound design and synthesis fascinate me because they represent the perfect fusion of technical precision and creative expression.

My electrical engineering foundation gives me a deep understanding of how signals flow through circuits, filters shape frequencies, and oscillators generate the building blocks of sound. This technical knowledge allows me to approach synthesis not just as a user, but as someone who truly understands what's happening under the hood.

Whether I'm designing custom patches, experimenting with modular synthesis, or crafting soundscapes, I'm always exploring the boundaries between the mathematical beauty of signal processing and the emotional impact of music.
