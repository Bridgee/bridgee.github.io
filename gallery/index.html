About My Photography

Photography serves as both documentation and artistic expression in my life. I'm drawn to vintage lenses because they capture something beyond just sharp images - they capture a vibe, a mood, a feeling that modern optics often miss.

There's something magical about the way vintage glass renders light, the subtle imperfections, the character that comes from decades of engineering history. These lenses help me see the world with a different perspective, adding warmth and soul to moments worth preserving.
